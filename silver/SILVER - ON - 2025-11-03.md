# ON — fiscal 2025-Q3 call (2025-11-03)

**The key idea:** onsemi is threading a needle between "we're stabilizing" and "we're not recovering yet" — real sequential growth in Auto/Industrial and a genuinely emerging AI data-center power franchise, but management is careful to attribute every beat to project-ramp lumpiness rather than demand, and flatly states no restocking cycle has begun. The subtext is a bet that AI power delivery (Treo, vertical GaN, SiC JFET, the Vcore acquisition) becomes the next growth leg before the legacy Auto/Industrial cycle turns.

## The read — 3-5 points from the whole transcript

1. **"Stabilization" is the load-bearing word, and it's doing a lot of work.** CEO Hassane El-Khoury used it to describe Auto (+7%) and Industrial (+5%) sequential growth while simultaneously warning "we haven't seen a restocking cycle yet" — the company wants credit for a bottom without promising a recovery.
2. **AI data center is the first real second growth engine, sized for the first time.** AI revenue is "almost $250 million" for 2025, roughly doubling y/y each quarter, and El-Khoury claims onsemi is one of only two vendors able to power a rack "from wall to core" — a claim analysts pushed on given ~13 named competitors in the space.
3. **The Nexperia crisis is treated as a non-event, but the CEO's own words undercut that.** Asked directly about impact, El-Khoury said "it's too soon to call anything," yet moments later admitted the industry has been running with inventory "below critical levels" for two years — a supply shock landing on an already-thin channel is exactly the mechanism he's been warning about.
4. **Capital allocation is bifurcated: return cash now, build optionality for AI later.** $925M in buybacks YTD (~100% of FCF) sits alongside a fresh acquisition (Vcore/Aura IP) and a new fab technology (vertical GaN) — the company isn't sacrificing shareholder returns to fund the AI bet, it's doing both.
5. **Lead times pushed out to ~20 weeks despite demand merely stabilizing** — a sign that channel inventory is thinner than the "stabilization" framing implies, consistent with the CFO's die-bank building "for the mass market" and CEO's warning about industry-wide low inventory.

## Economy & consumer
- **Sequential growth without a restocking signal:** Auto +7%, Industrial +5% q/q, but "we haven't seen a restocking cycle yet" (El-Khoury) — the growth is described as ramp timing, not renewed demand.
- **Geographic divergence flags macro softness in Europe/China:** Europe down 4% ("macro softness persisted"), China down 7% sequentially, while Americas +22% and Japan +38% — though management attributes some of the swing to a large customer shifting order placement between regions, not underlying demand shift.
- **OEM restocking gated on consumer confidence:** "What they need to see, one is a credible demand signal... consumer level confidence... consumer level demand signal that people are going to buy cars" — restocking is explicitly conditioned on end-consumer confidence that hasn't yet materialized.

## The business — what's working, what's not
- **Working: AI data center franchise scaling fast.** AI revenue roughly doubled y/y for three straight quarters, reaching ~$250M for 2025; design wins now span solar/ESS, UPS, rack PSU, and compute-board smart power stages.
- **Working: disciplined capital return.** $372M free cash flow in Q3, 21% FCF margin YTD, and ~100% of YTD FCF returned via $925M in buybacks.
- **Not working / structural drag: noncore exits continue to suppress reported revenue** — ~$45M exited in Q3, ~$55M more expected in Q4, and roughly 5% of 2025 revenue "doesn't repeat" into 2026, per CFO Thad Trent.
- **Margin still utilization-dependent, not demand-dependent:** gross margin beat (38%) came from mix, not volume; utilization rose to 74% only because of proactive die-bank building, and is expected to fall back in Q4.

## Investing & scarcity
- **New technology investment: vertical GaN (vGaN) on proprietary GaN-on-GaN in the Syracuse fab**, reducing energy loss "up to 50%," already sampling with lead AI and automotive customers; revenue contribution not expected until 2027.
- **Vcore/Aura Semiconductor IP acquisition** closes a gap in multiphase controllers and smart power stages for AI compute platforms, with sampling this quarter and production in early 2026 — a "time to market" bridge before being redesigned onto the Treo architecture.
- **Scarcity is inventory depth, not fab capacity:** El-Khoury says "inventory is low for the last 2 years, and we're draining inventory below critical levels" industry-wide; lead times have pushed out to ~20 weeks even as demand only stabilizes — a channel primed for disruption, which the Nexperia situation may be demonstrating in real time.

- **Belief: AI data center will keep growing and onsemi will keep gaining share.** Action: concrete — Vcore acquisition, vGaN sampling with lead customers, doubled Treo product sampling on track, collaboration with NVIDIA on 800V architecture. - **Belief: replenishment/restocking cycle is coming.** Action: none yet — company is building its own die-bank inventory for the mass market but is not describing customer-side restocking as underway, and calls it a lesson from COVID still unresolved industry-wide.
