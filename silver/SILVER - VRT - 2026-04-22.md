# VRT — fiscal 2026-Q1 call (2026-04-22)

**The key idea:** Vertiv posted blowout Americas-driven growth (44% organic) while EMEA cratered 29%, and the entire raised full-year guide hinges on two things not yet visible in the numbers: EMEA "uncoiling" in H2 and new manufacturing capacity that "starts to hit us in the second half." This is a story about a company converting an AI infrastructure supercycle into share gain and margin expansion, betting the back half of the year on capacity coming online and a region that's currently shrinking.

## The read — 3-5 points from the whole transcript
1. **Growth is a one-region story so far.** Americas organic sales grew 44% while EMEA fell 29% organically; Q1's headline 23% organic growth masks a market that is almost entirely a US hyperscale/colo/neocloud phenomenon today. CEO Gio Albertazzi conceded enterprise remains "still a little bit far away" as an independent growth driver.
2. **The full-year guide requires an H2 inflection that hasn't happened yet.** Management is guiding to ~30% full-year organic growth against ~22-23% in H1, explicitly attributing the gap to capacity coming online and EMEA/APAC "acceleration" — both are forward promises, not Q1 results. CFO Craig Chamberlin: capacity ramp creates "a little bit of headwinds" quarter-over-quarter into Q2 even as it's the growth engine for H2.
3. **Convergence and prefabrication are the real product strategy, not just packaging.** Gio repeatedly framed SmartRun/OneCore as system-level, pre-engineered convergence plays ("not just prefabrication... convergence of our solution into a system") — the thesis is that owning power+thermal+IT as one integrated sale is a moat that "is very difficult to replicate."
4. **Balance sheet is loaded for M&A, not returns.** Net leverage of 0.2x after three acquisitions (PurgeRite, ThermoKey, BMarko) in recent quarters, with Gio stating "we have no fixed limits" on check size for the right technology asset — capital deployment priority is clearly M&A and organic capacity/R&D over buybacks or dividends.
5. **800-volt DC and behind-the-meter power are being treated as 2027 stories, deliberately deferred.** Both liquid cooling TAM expansion (from 800V architecture) and bring-your-own-power (partnerships with Caterpillar, Oklo, CPower Energy) are framed as real but not yet monetizing — management is investing engineering now for a payoff explicitly pushed past this year.

## Economy & consumer
- **No macro or consumer commentary at all** — this is a pure infrastructure supplier call; the "demand environment" discussed is AI data center capex, not broader economic conditions.
- **Tariffs are treated as manageable friction, not a threat.** Dave Cote: "Tariffs, supply chain complexity, labor constraints. These are real, but they're manageable... they raise the bar in ways that favor established players like us." Management claims to already be **price-cost positive net of Section 122/232 tariff impact** as of end of Q1.

## The business — what's working, what's not
- **Americas is carrying the entire company.** $1.81B in sales, up 53% reported/44% organic, with margins benefiting from "operational leverage, disciplined execution, and commercial intensity."
- **EMEA is a genuine problem being reframed as a coiled spring.** Sales down 29% organically with margin deleverage; management attributes it to "softer orders that we saw in Q2 and Q3 of 2025" and claims Q1 bookings and pipeline acceleration support a second-half recovery — but this is asserted, not yet shown in reported sales.
- **APAC growth (12% organic) came in below guidance** "primarily due to timing," a soft explanation for a miss that management didn't press further on.
- **Services growth parity with products is partly acquisition-driven**, per CFO: "If you look at organic, you're feeling the impact of PurgeRite there as well" — a transparency point worth noting since it tempers the services-momentum narrative.

## Investing & scarcity
- **Capacity is the explicit binding constraint.** Gio: growth confidence for H2 rests on "capacity... a lot of that capacity starts to hit us in the second half," alongside Q4 backlog and observed shortening of some customer-requested lead times.
- **CapEx "sustainably higher" than a year ago**, funding organic manufacturing expansion (concentrated in the Americas) plus services headcount and customer-witness-test lab capacity — test capacity specifically flagged as "a growth enabler" given the complexity of new data center technology qualification.
- **M&A as a capacity/capability lever, not diversification** — PurgeRite (fluid/liquid cooling services), ThermoKey (heat exchange/dry coolers for EMEA), BMarko (structural fabrication) all directly extend manufacturing/services capacity or technical scope rather than entering new markets.

## Where they think it's going vs what they're doing about it
- **Belief: EMEA snaps back in H2.** Action so far: increased conviction language and Q1 bookings cited as evidence, but EMEA sales were still down 29% organically in the reported quarter — the "spring uncoiling" is a forecast, not yet a result.
- **Belief: 800-volt DC architecture and liquid cooling TAM expansion are coming.** Action: prototype/validation work underway, portfolio launches targeted for H2 2026, but Gio explicitly pushed actual shipping to customers to 2027 — engineering investment now, revenue deferred, and he declined to elaborate further ("a little bit premature").
- **Belief: enterprise AI adoption will eventually be a real Vertiv growth driver beyond cloud/colo.** Action: none disclosed yet — Gio directly said this impact is "probably a little bit still far away," an honest gap between long-term thesis and near-term commitment.
- **Belief: behind-the-meter/bring-your-own-power reshapes data center design.** Action: partnerships already in place (Caterpillar, Oklo, CPower Energy) positioning Vertiv across microgrids, battery storage, and power train integration — this is one area where stated belief and current action are actually aligned, not just deferred.

- **No acquisition price tags for ThermoKey, BMarko, or PurgeRite** — CFO confirmed sizes weren't disclosed and implied materiality thresholds weren't triggered.
- **Declined to give firm 800-volt DC shipping timeline** beyond "second half this year launches" for the portfolio and actual customer shipping pushed to 2027, calling further detail "premature."
