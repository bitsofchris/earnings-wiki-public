# IDXX — fiscal 2026-Q1 call (2026-05-05)

**The key idea:** IDEXX beat and raised on the back of volume and innovation execution, not price or a healed consumer — wellness visits are still declining and fuel/macro uncertainty remains unresolved, but management is betting that an aging pandemic-era pet cohort and a wave of new diagnostic modalities (InViewDX FNA, Cancer Dx, Catalyst menu expansion) can keep outrunning a soft clinical-visit backdrop. This is also a leadership handoff call: outgoing CEO Jay Mazelsky's last, with incoming CEO Michael Erickson previewing an AI-forward strategy to be detailed at an August Investor Day.

1. **Growth is volume-and-innovation-led, not price-led.** Net price realization held flat at ~4% (unchanged from prior guide); the entire 70bps guidance raise came from volume — diagnostic frequency, new menu uptake (Catalyst pancreatic lipase/cortisol), and instrument install-base growth. Management's counter-narrative — pandemic-adopted dogs (Doodles, Frenchies) aging into heavier medical needs — is real but still early-innings ("this is now the third quarter that we've seen that").

5. **CEO transition is being handled with continuity messaging, not strategic reset.** Jay Mazelsky moves to Executive Chair; incoming CEO Michael Erickson previewed AI as a stated priority (already embedded in InViewDX and ezyVet) but deferred specifics to an August Investor Day — a soft commitment with no numbers attached yet.

## Economy & consumer
- **Wellness visits remain the drag, non-well visits are stabilizing.** US same-store clinical visits were -1% in Q1 (better than the initial guide by "about a point"), with wellness down -3% offsetting non-well visits up 20bps YoY.
- **Fuel/macro is an acknowledged unresolved risk, not a modeled certainty.** Emerson: "it's a pretty volatile and evolving dynamic... a little bit hard to predict." The full-year -1.5% visit guide is explicitly a trailing-12-month average, not a forecast of improvement.
- **Aging pandemic-cohort pets are the explicit bull case for volume.** Jay Mazelsky ties the improving non-well trend to breeds ("Doodles... Frenchies") adopted in the pandemic boom now aging into medical need, validated anecdotally by corporate customer data — a real but still-forming signal.

- **International (+12% organic) is outgrowing the US** on the strength of a multi-year localization strategy (ProCyte One, VetConnect PLUS, Leishmania testing) — Mazelsky calls it "not just... commercial expansions" but a full ecosystem build, not merely favorable macro.
- **Rapid assay revenue was flat**, cannibalized by customers migrating pancreatic lipase testing onto the Catalyst platform — an internal product-mix headwind (~2%) rather than a competitive loss.
- **Water business hit supply-chain disruption in the Middle East**, a modest but explicit regional headwind already baked into guidance.
