# TSLA — 2026-Q2 call (2026-07-22)

**The key idea:** Tesla is mid-metamorphosis from carmaker into an AI-physical-infrastructure company — customers now buy "Full Self-Driving with a car attached" — funding five simultaneous manufacturing ramps (Robotaxi, Optimus, its own chip fab, solar, grid batteries) by going into debt for the first time at scale, with Musk explicitly choosing speed over capital efficiency: "a little less capital efficient… sooner… is the higher NPV outcome."

## Where they're going / what they're building

- **The car is becoming a subscription.** FSD is the stated demand driver — over half of North American deliveries activate it at delivery, ~1.5M paid customers, purchase option *removed* in most markets to force subscriptions. Recurring software revenue quietly replacing the one-time car sale.
- **Robotaxi: breadth first, then the flood.** 380k unsupervised miles, "zero notable incidents," >10%/week growth — deliberately spread across seven markets in *dozens* of vehicles. Stated reasons: prove the stack generalizes ("time to launch a new city trends toward zero — toward operating entire states"), work out kinks small, per-city regulation. Only named constraint: "the march of nines of reliability." Camera-only victory lap explicit: no LiDAR, no HD maps, "the so-called experts" wrong.
- **Optimus: the biggest bet, most honest risk disclosure.** "The hardest product to scale manufacturing we've ever made — everything on the robot is new. There is no supply chain." In-housing; Fremont line replacing S/X; Optimus 4 in Austin aims *an order of magnitude* beyond Optimus 3 (aspirationally 10M/yr vs 1M/yr). Training flywheel: watch factory workers → internet video → "Optimus Academy" RL. Same pixels-in-controls-out stack and team as FSD.
- **Digital Optimus — the computer-use agent, from the driving side.** Drives a screen "the same way you drive a car" (real-time video in, controls out), managed by SpaceX's Grok, packaged into **Megapods** (Tesla AI computer + x86, Megapack-style) deployable at Superchargers (~7GW distributed power). A distributed-AI-compute play nobody was pricing.
- **Terafab: chip sovereignty.** Own fab (announcement pending, "deserves its own day"); Austin dev fab with lithography-to-packaging under one roof; AI5 into Optimus mid-next-year; AI6 claimed "best edge computing chip in the world." Stated reason: without it "we simply won't have enough AI chips" for Optimus.
- **Energy as the AI power play.** Sharpest technical nugget of the call: training-run power draw can drop 70% for 100ms — hyperscalers can't smooth that, batteries can (why SpaceX buys Megapacks). Macro claim: US generation is ~2.5× average usage — "you could double the energy output of the United States just with batteries." Order-of-magnitude solar-manufacturing build, silicon-to-panel.
- **SpaceX entanglement thickening** — investment + framework agreement, Grok in car and managing Digital Optimus, Starlink into every vehicle (Robotaxis can't tolerate cellular "Bermuda Triangles"), Terafab collaboration; combination question got a procedural non-denial ("appropriate process"). Relevant: SPCX now in our universe.

## What's changing

- **The constraint flipped from demand to supply.** Record deliveries, biggest order backlog since 2023 — production "limited by our supply chain," batteries *and* electronic components. Micron publicly thanked for a memory allocation "on reasonable terms given the pretty insane pricing of memory."
- **Same funding-model shift as Meta, one week earlier.** Capex more than doubled sequentially, FCF negative, debt capacity up to $30B secured — Tesla's first big borrow of this era.
- **Margins compressing everywhere at once**: auto ex-credits down (flat only after excusing one-offs), energy gross margin roughly *halved* with a lower normalization target, rate-subvention costs biting, R&D swelling on four pre-production ramps.
- **Earnings quality:** net income propped by ~$1B mark-to-market gain on SpaceX holdings — non-operating.

## What's NOT working

- **Quality escape in energy**: ~$240M warranty true-up for vendor cell issues on legacy deployments.
- **Robotaxi fleet size is the visible gap** — pressed directly (dozens, not hundreds); answers reasonable (generalization, kinks, Cybercab chassis data, city-by-city regs) but the exponential is in *miles per car*, not cars.
- **Regulation is a patchwork grind**: New Jersey "disheartening," no federal framework, state sensor-requirement rules a live threat to the camera-only thesis.
- **Semi autonomy deprioritized** ~6 months — the march of nines eats everything else.
- **Litigation charges again**; FX and Bitcoin dings.
- Meta-risk from Musk himself: every impressive humanoid demo in the industry is "pre-programmed or remote controlled" — the product must surpass that *and then* be mass-manufactured with no supply chain.
