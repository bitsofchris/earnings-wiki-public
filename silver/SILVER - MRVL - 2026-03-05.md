# MRVL — fiscal 2026-Q4 call (2026-03-05)

**The key idea:** Marvell used this call to reset expectations upward for the third consecutive quarter — fiscal 2027 revenue guidance has gone from ~$9.5B (Sept) to ~$10B (Dec) to ~$11B (now), entirely on the back of hyperscaler AI infrastructure spend, while simultaneously closing two acquisitions (Celestial AI, XConn) that bet the company's next growth leg is "scale-up" optical/electrical interconnect rather than custom silicon alone. The tension underneath: management insists the growth is broad-based across four hyperscalers and multiple product lines, but the multiplier on nearly every upward revision is a single high-stakes custom XPU program and a still-unproven CPO technology.

## The read — 3-5 points from the whole transcript

1. **Three upward revisions in five months.** CEO Matt Murphy walked through the raise explicitly: "$9.4-ish" in September, "more like 10" in December, "now I'm saying it's more like 11" — driven almost entirely by interconnect bookings that were "conservatively" plugged to capex growth but now track more closely to actual GPU/XPU unit growth.
2. **Interconnect reclassified from capex-proxy to unit-proxy.** Murphy conceded the prior modeling approach undersold the business: "you're plugging your interconnect business at CapEx, but it really looks more like it should be tied to GPU, XPU. And that's really the case" — interconnect growth guidance jumped from 30% to >50% YoY for fiscal 2027 on this repricing alone.
3. **M&A is a straight bet on scale-up optical networking.** Celestial AI (photonic fabric/CPO) and XConn (PCIe/CXL switching) were funded by automotive Ethernet divestiture proceeds and are explicitly not expected to move revenue until fiscal 2028 ($250M combined) — this is capital committed years ahead of proven revenue, on a market Marvell itself says "could exceed $10 billion by 2030."
4. **Management pushed back hard on bearish custom-silicon narrative.** Asked why Marvell doesn't shift resources entirely into higher-margin interconnect, Murphy's answer ran long and heated: "we're in that business. Our customers are counting on us... do you see me blinking? You don't." The defensiveness itself is a signal that the "is custom XPU concentration risk" question is landing.
5. **Guidance floor, not ceiling, on EPS.** The $5+ non-GAAP EPS for fiscal 2028 was explicitly called "not a prescriptive number... just a 5-plus" — deliberately soft-pedaled even as revenue guidance ($15B) was given with much more confidence, suggesting margin/opex assumptions are the part management is least sure of.

## Economy & consumer

No macroeconomic or consumer commentary — Marvell is a pure infrastructure supplier and the call is entirely about hyperscaler capital spending intentions. Notable by omission: no discussion of the broader chip cycle, tariffs, or China exposure, topics that show up on many other semiconductor calls this quarter.

## The business — what's working, what's not

- **Record quarter, broad-based growth.** Q4 revenue $2.219B (+7% sequential, +22% YoY), full-year data center revenue crossed $6B (+46% YoY); "we achieved sequential growth across all key product lines, including optical interconnects, custom silicon, switching and storage."
- **Custom silicon doubled and is guided to double again.** From zero to $1.5B in fiscal 2026, expected to grow "north of 20%" in fiscal 2027 and at least double again in fiscal 2028 — but CFO/CEO repeatedly declined to give exact figures, keeping the growth rate qualitative.
- **Switching is a genuine new leg.** Data center switch revenue guided to surpass $600M in fiscal 2027 (raised from $500M last quarter), with 51.2T ramping and 100T sampling in H1.
- **Non-GAAP operating margin at 35.7%,** up meaningfully YoY, with EPS growth (+81% for the year) running roughly 2x revenue growth — real operating leverage, not just topline momentum.

## Investing & scarcity

- **Celestial AI + XConn acquisitions** closed and immediately integrated into joint roadmap discussions — Celestial's photonic fabric CPO chiplets target a $500M annualized run rate by Q4 fiscal 2028, doubling to $1B by Q4 fiscal 2029, first commercial deployment for scale-up connectivity at one large customer next year.
- **Wafer commitments already exceed disclosed guidance** for the new Tier-1 XPU program — Murphy said the "rough plug" in guidance is "significantly lower than actually the wafers that we're planning on starting" — a deliberately conservative public number sitting atop more aggressive internal manufacturing commitments.

## Where they think it's going vs what they're doing about it

Management believes cloud capex growth continues accelerating through fiscal 2027 before "moderating" in fiscal 2028 — yet Marvell's own data-center growth guidance for fiscal 2028 (~50%) is *higher* than fiscal 2027, justified by CPO/custom lines maturing independent of the capex cycle. The action lines up: capacity reservations, two acquisitions funded before revenue exists to justify them, and a UALink/CXL switch roadmap being built ahead of a market (scale-up interconnect) that doesn't fully exist commercially yet. The gap: guidance for the new Tier-1 XPU customer is deliberately lowballed relative to actual wafer starts — a hedge against a program delay that would strand committed manufacturing capacity.
