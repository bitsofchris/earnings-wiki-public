# BIIB — fiscal 2025-Q4 call (2026-02-06)

**The key idea:** Biogen closed FY25 above guidance on the back of newly-branded "growth products" finally outpacing MS decline, but FY26 guidance calls for revenue to fall again as legacy erosion (Tecfidera generics, new Tysabri biosimilar) outruns the growth franchise for one more year. The real bet is a rebuilt late-stage pipeline — ten phase 3 programs, nearly all added in the last twelve months — that management is funding entirely by reallocating legacy-business cash rather than expanding spend, with the earliest new launches (SLE, AMR) not arriving until 2028.

## Economy & consumer

## The business — what's working, what's not
- **Growth products crossed a real threshold:** in Q4, growth-product revenue (including reclassified Vumerity) **exceeded** MS-business revenue for the first time, hitting $3.3B for FY25, up 19%.
- **Zurzuvae "has really been a surprise to all of us"** — sales more than doubled in 2025, with only ~80,000 of an estimated 500,000 annual postpartum depression sufferers diagnosed, a market CEO argues is still being created, not just captured.
- **MS erosion is set to worsen, not stabilize:** FY26 will see a full year of Tecfidera EU generic erosion (versus ~6 months in FY25) plus a newly launched Tysabri biosimilar in the EU, pushing MS-ex-Vumerity revenue guidance to mid-teens decline.
- **Skyclarys' quarter included a one-time $9M favorable US inventory build (to reverse in Q1) and a $12M unfavorable ex-US pricing true-up** — CFO Robin Kramer flagged both as noise, a useful reminder that low-volume/high-value rare-disease revenue is genuinely lumpy quarter to quarter.

## Investing & scarcity
- **Prelaunch investment for lupus/nephrology is framed as capability-building, not big OpEx expansion:** Biogen is recruiting commercial/medical talent from companies with existing rheumatology/nephrology/transplant presence rather than building full new teams, with Viehbacher citing Sanofi's Dupixent expansion into indications it had no prior franchise in as the playbook.
- **Capital is explicitly being reallocated from legacy MS toward growth/pipeline products**, per CFO Kramer, rather than sourced from new spend — core OpEx guided flat year over year for 2026 despite the new launches ahead.
- **Business development remains bounded and frustrated:** Viehbacher said the company is looking daily for acquisitions in the ~$5-6B range (post-phase-3, early commercialization) but "it's hard to find things that actually generate value for our shareholders" at a price that makes sense.
