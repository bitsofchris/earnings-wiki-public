# META — 2026-Q2 call (2026-07-29)

**The key idea:** Meta is spending ahead of receipts on two linked bets — that *personal agents* become the next billion-person platform with **WhatsApp as its front door**, and that *owning the full stack including models* is the durable moat — while the funding quietly shifts from own cash to debt and partners, and the income statement absorbs the bruises.

## Where they're going / what they're building

- **Personal agents = the flagship bet, still unshipped.** Zuckerberg: "extremely unlikely" that in five years billions of people *don't* have an agent working 24/7 on their health, finances, relationships, career. Candid on today's state: proto-agents "take a bunch of fiddling… you have to get into a terminal… maybe it breaks down over time" — Meta's bet is being the one that "just works" for billions. Verbatim: *"we haven't done that yet."*
- **WhatsApp positioned as the agent interface.** Leading Meta AI surface; new Head of WhatsApp hired from Indian payments (commerce tell); incognito mode shipped (chats even Meta can't see) as trust groundwork; WhatsApp paid messaging pushed non-ads consumer revenue over $1B/quarter for the first time. Messaging apps = the platform "where we all interact with multiple agents."
- **Business agents → "business-in-a-box."** 1M+ businesses weekly; roadmap read aloud: agent talks to customers → digests overnight → suggests growth moves → competitive intelligence → "start and run a whole business" on Meta rails. Pricing converges on the ads model: pay for results, then "an efficient auction over our compute."
- **Compute becomes a product — reluctantly.** BlackRock 1GW JV; premium offers for capacity; doctrine explicit: "significantly higher margin on selling intelligence rather than selling compute."
- **Model strategy: two-tier + sovereignty + open-source return.** Efficient models serve billions of prompts, frontier models the hard problems. Open-weight releases coming back ("not dogmatic"). The sovereignty answer was the most revealing of the call: relying on others' open models carries "risk in that reliance"; Meta is "a full stack technology company" and models are now part of the stack; models have "different combinations of skills, kind of like people" — you tune your own.
- **An app factory is forming.** Forum (groups), Seller (marketplace), Instagram Instants — AI makes shipping standalone apps cheap; the recommendation engine is the distribution that scales them (the Threads playbook, industrialized).
- **Glasses = the superintelligence hardware**, first to ship with the model built in; Connect Sept 23 next reveal.

## What's changing

- **The funding model.** FCF ~zero; debt mix deliberately rising ("bring down our cost of capital"); BlackRock JV is a *template*. The era of funding AI from ad cash flow alone ended this quarter.
- **Capex floor raised again**, expense floor raised (legal), tax outlook raised — every revision went the expensive direction. Refused to guide 2027 capex; near-term capacity declared more valuable than long-term; 2028 = land-and-power optionality only.
- **Fewer, pricier people.** Headcount down 3% while comp grows on AI-talent hiring.
- **The recommender is becoming an LLM.** Every public Instagram post runs through an LLM; continuously pre-training a large model on recommendations data with "healthy scaling laws"; over half of recommended Feed content now under a day old (doubled in a year); users can prompt their own algorithm (Your Algo / Shape Your Feed, ~80% retention among users who engage).
- **Meta AI engagement jumped ~60%** since the new model integration.

## What's NOT working

- **The scissors: expenses growing at ~2× revenue's rate.** Operating income *declined* despite a strong top line (positive only ex-charges). EPS missed the street badly; stock paid.
- **Over-hired and admitted it** — ~8,000 severance in May.
- **Own models don't carry the load yet** — "third-party AI token costs" named as an expense driver.
- **Consumer perception gap, said to the CEO's face:** regular people think AI is "a glorified search tool." The fix (agents that just work) doesn't exist yet — the flagship consumer thesis is promissory.
- **Legal is structural now** — multi-billion charge, youth trials with "material loss" language, moved the guidance.
- **Quest fading** (glasses up, headsets down).

## Street anxiety (compressed)

Prove ROIC on this pipeline soon · how is 2027 funded, debt/equity philosophy · enterprise selling is "a new muscle" (his admission) — do they have it · will the star lab visibly ship · why buy compute while fielding offers to sell it ("nowhere near enough compute for all the demand").
