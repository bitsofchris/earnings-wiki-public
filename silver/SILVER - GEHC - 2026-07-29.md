# GEHC — fiscal 2026-Q2 call (2026-07-29)

**The key idea:** A record orders quarter and a CFO exit landed in the same call, and management spent most of its energy explaining why one doesn't undercut the other. The real story is Patient Care Solutions — an entire segment running at negative margin from supply-chain fumbles — now formally up for sale or restructuring while the rest of the portfolio (imaging, diagnostics) carries the company.

## Where they're going / what they're building
- **AI-enabled hardware upgrades sold as subscriptions**, not one-time purchases: True Definition DL, a deep-learning CT software upgrade, "enables nearly double the image resolution across the installed base" and is offered as a subscription, extending the AIR Recon DL playbook of monetizing the existing fleet rather than only new boxes.
- **Platform reuse is now the explicit margin lever.** The CFO singled out Vivid Pioneer ultrasound as proof: "a lower manufactured cost, coupled with differentiated AI capabilities that drive higher pricing," delivering "a significantly higher gross margin than the prior platform" — and called this development model "replicated across all of our products in our new wave of innovation."
- **Disease-state bundling over product-by-product selling.** Two large new accounts (Catholic Health in New York, ~$500M; University Hospital Essen in Germany, oncology/theranostics) were both won by leading with clinical roadmaps, not equipment specs — "technology was an enabler, not a starting point."
- **Radiopharma remains a slow-build bet.** Flyrcado hit 545 weekly doses, "approximately a 40% increase over April levels," with new customer onboarding driving the ramp; management held the $500M-by-2028 target while noting newly onboarded accounts take roughly 60 days to become productive.
- **"AI Inside" as a margin lever for the company itself** — using AI internally to cut G&A as revenue scales, explicitly framed as agents and tools reducing headcount growth relative to revenue growth.
- Photon-counting CT (Photonova Spectra) is real but not yet a revenue driver — CE marking slipped to second-half 2026 (later than originally communicated) and the CEO was explicit: "it's not a major driver within the orders book, will be in the future."

## What's changing
- **CFO Jay Saccaro is leaving** after three years, replaced on an interim basis by 38-year company veteran George Newcomb, with a search "just kicked off." Framed by both sides as an opportunity, not a rift, but an analyst called the timing "a bit abrupt," and specifics on the destination were never given.
- **Patient Care Solutions is formally under strategic review** — "continued ownership, a sale, and other value-enhancing transactions" all on the table — after the segment posted a 13.5% organic revenue decline and negative segment EBIT.
- **Commercial reorganization (AIS, Global Markets) is credited for the order surge**: management pointed to improved deal-visibility and win-rate as a direct, repeatable output of the restructuring, not one-off deal timing.
- **Price/cost actions taken in Q2 are expected to compound**: management guided to over 300 basis points of EBIT margin expansion from first half to second half, driven by these actions plus higher-margin new-product mix.
- Segment reporting itself changed — Imaging and Advanced Visualization Solutions were combined into "Advanced Imaging Solutions" this quarter, with historicals recast.

## What's NOT working (or being talked around)
- **PCS supply chain broke down in-quarter**: "short on supply of some critical components" caused an inability to fulfill orders already won — a self-inflicted operational failure, not a demand problem, and orders in the segment were actually strong.
- **Guidance was not raised despite a clean tariff-refund and tax beat.** EPS got $0.04 from tariff refunds and $0.02 from a lower tax rate, yet full-year EPS guidance stayed at $4.80–$5. The CFO's own framing — "others have taken more" — is a tacit admission peers were more aggressive marking up guidance off similar one-time items; GEHC chose to bank the cushion instead.
- **China is guided down again, no improvement assumed.** Management still assumes "a year-over-year decline in 2026" from expanding VBP (volume-based procurement) pricing pressure and called the current dynamics "not new or out of the ordinary" — a phrase that reads as resignation more than confidence.
- **Inflation (memory chips, oil, freight) cost 120 basis points in the quarter**, in line with plan, but management's own language — "very volatile macro environment," a $250M full-year assumption carrying "cushion" — signals this is being actively managed down rather than resolved.
- **No specifics on generic Omnipaque competition beyond "haven't seen any impact at this point"** — a reassurance, not evidence, on a name-brand contrast-media franchise facing AB-rated generic entry.
