## The business — what's working, what's not
- **Working: breadth across end markets absorbed the Federal and Higher Ed weakness.** Commercial +10%, State & Local double-digit, K-12 +3% (client-device pull-forward ahead of price hikes), International +18% in USD (UK high-single-digit, Canada double-digit local currency) — all offset Federal's low-single-digit decline (shutdown-driven procurement delays) and "extended decision-making" in Higher Ed.
- **Not working: margin mix.** Gross margin fell 60bps to 21%; ex-netted-down core margin (per an analyst's math, confirmed by Al Miralles) was roughly flat year-over-year at 14.8% but down sequentially, reflecting normal Q1 seasonal trough in channel incentive earn-out plus lower services mix.
- **SG&A grew faster than operating income** (+8.8% vs. +1.8% non-GAAP operating income), with management attributing part of the gap to upfront AI-tooling and training investment intended to pay back in H2.
- **Inventory jumped 40%+ sequentially** as CDW "stepped up" to secure supply for customers ahead of price increases — Al Miralles says this was ASP-driven more than volume-driven and didn't meaningfully strain the cash conversion cycle (16 days, still inside target range).

## Investing & scarcity
- **Geared for Growth**: a multi-year AI-powered operating-model rewire (quote-to-cash modernization, embedded AI in core workflows) targeting $100M gross annual run-rate savings by 2027, rising toward $200M by 2028, with roughly half reinvested back into growth initiatives.
- **Boost Run partnership**: gives customers GPU-as-a-service access "composable with on-premises and cloud environments" — a direct response to accelerated-compute supply outstripping demand across customers beyond hyperscalers.
- **The binding constraint is access to compute, not customer appetite**: "the push to deploy AI at scale has driven demand for accelerating compute past available supply, making access, not ambition, a crucial restraint" — CDW is positioning itself as the access broker.
- **Memory pricing/supply is the acute near-term scarcity**, actively reshaping what customers buy quarter to quarter.

- **Action**: guidance raised only for Q2 (backlog-driven), full-year and H2 view held flat — management is acting on what's already in backlog, not underwriting its own normalization thesis.
